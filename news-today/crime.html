Gujarat: Tenant Arrested for Allegedly Permitting Assault on Wife and Minor Daughter Over Unpaid Rent

In a shocking case from Morbi, Gujarat, a man has been arrested after he allegedly permitted his landlord and the landlord's relative to sexually assault his wife and 13-year-old daughter after failing to pay rent. The victim's mother — the maternal grandmother of the minor — lodged a complaint against her son-in-law under the POCSO Act and several sections of the BNS at Morbi City A Division police station.

The landlord (55) was produced in court and sent for judicial remand. He was subsequently taken into judicial custody after his remand period ended. The tenant has also been arrested and is in jail. YB Jadeja, Inspector, Morbi A Division, confirmed both arrests. The landlord's relative is yet to be arrested and police are actively searching for him. The landlord's family members are also reported to be absconding.

Police have completed the panchnama of the crime scene and conducted medical examinations of both the victims and the accused. Forensic evidence has been collected, though its exact nature has not been disclosed.

According to the complainant, the family had moved to Morbi just six months ago due to professional commitments. The husband rented a house for ₹2,000 per month and attempted to establish his business, which eventually collapsed. As the unpaid rent accumulated, he allegedly struck the arrangement with the landlord. The assaults reportedly took place on multiple occasions at the rented house in Tankara.
