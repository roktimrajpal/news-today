Bollywood

Shah Rukh Khan Announces 'Fireworks' — Sanjay Leela Bhansali Epic to Begin Shoot in October

In a surprise Instagram live, Shah Rukh Khan confirmed his much-anticipated return to cinema with 'Fireworks', a sweeping action-romance epic directed by Sanjay Leela Bhansali. This will be the first collaboration between the superstar and India's most visually lavish filmmaker.

'Fireworks' is set across 1940s colonial Bombay and present-day Mumbai, following a dynasty of musicians caught between war, love, and partition. The film will be shot across India, Spain, and Morocco and is budgeted at ₹600 crore, making it the most expensive Indian production ever greenlit.

Deepika Padukone is confirmed as female lead, with Ranbir Kapoor in a pivotal supporting role. Music will be composed by AR Rahman. Release is scheduled for Eid 2028.

Indian Film 'Mitti' Wins Palme d'Or at Cannes — First Indian Film to Claim Top Prize Since 1946

Anurag Kashyap's 'Mitti' (Soil) made history at Cannes 2026, winning the Palme d'Or — the most coveted prize in world cinema. The 3-hour saga, shot entirely in rural Bihar, follows three generations of farmers battling drought, debt, and displacement. The jury called it "a timeless portrait of human endurance."
